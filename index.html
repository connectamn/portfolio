Research & Thesis

Undergraduate Thesis 2025

Early Detection of Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) Using Ensemble Machine Learning Techniques

Abstract

This thesis explores ensemble machine learning for early Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) prediction using clinical, biomarker, and lifestyle data. After feature engineering and risk stratification, the stacking ensemble reached 94.2% accuracy, demonstrating high promise for non-invasive, affordable clinical screening tools.
