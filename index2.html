Undergraduate Research

Thesis and ongoing academic investigations

Undergraduate Thesis

Optimizing Deep Learning Architectures for Edge Computing Devices in Medical Imaging

Abstract

Deploying neural networks to edge hardware with constrained compute capacity poses latency and memory challenges. This thesis proposes a lightweight convolutional network pruning framework coupled with dynamic quantization. Experimental results show a 42% reduction in memory footprint while maintaining 96.4% diagnostic accuracy on standard chest X-ray datasets.
